📅 Morning Routine

Take 5 minutes to check these vitals. If any "red flag" appears, call your nurse.

0 of 6 tasks complete
Weight Recorded Use the same scale, same time, wearing similar clothing. Report sudden gain (>1kg in 24h).
Blood Pressure Checked Sit quietly for 5 mins before testing. Report readings much higher or lower than your normal.
Drain Fluid Clarity Fluid should be clear/pale yellow. Red Flag: Cloudy or bloody fluid.
Exit Site Inspected Look for redness, swelling, or drainage. Red Flag: Pus or new pain.
Total UF Noted Check the cycler screen for Net Ultrafiltration. Record this in your logbook.
Fever/Chills Check Do you feel warm or shaky? Take your temperature if unsure.
