The "No-Touch" Technique

Your connection point is the gateway to your body. Learning to keep it sterile is your best defense against infection.

🛡️ Understanding the Sterile Zones

In PD, there are parts of the equipment that are "clean" and parts that are "sterile" (surgical-grade clean). The sterile zones must never touch anything else.

✅ Sterile Zones (Never Touch)

  • The tip of the catheter (transfer set)
  • The spike on the machine tubing
  • The inside of the pull-cap
  • The betadine sponge inside the disconnect cap

⚠️ Warning Zones

  • Your fingers (even after washing)
  • Furniture or bedding
  • Pet hair or dust
  • Your own clothing

🌙 Evening: Connecting

Follow these steps once your machine shows "Connect Patient".

Step 1
Final Check
Put on your mask. Verify your transfer set (the tube coming from your body) is closed.
Step 2
The Break-Away
Remove the old cap from your transfer set. Do not touch the exposed tip. Hold it firmly by the housing.
Step 3
The Spike
Pull the ring from the machine tubing (the patient line). In one smooth motion, screw it onto your transfer set.
Step 4
Open the Flow
Open the twist-clamp on your transfer set. Press "GO" on the machine. You are now connected for the night.

🚨 What if I touch the tip?

Accidents happen. If you accidentally touch the tip of your transfer set or it touches any surface (bedding, floor, table):

🛑

STOP IMMEDIATELY. Do not connect. Clamp your catheter. Screw on a fresh betadine cap and call your PD nurse immediately. They may need to put you on preventative antibiotics or swap your transfer set.

🌅 Morning: Disconnecting

When the cycler shows "End of Therapy".

Step 1
Close Clamps
Close the twist-clamp on your transfer set first. Then close the white clamp on the patient line.
Step 2
Prepare the Cap
Wash your hands again. Open a fresh disconnect cap (miniphase cap). Ensure the betadine sponge is inside.
Step 3
Swap & Seal
Unscrew the machine tubing from your transfer set. Immediately screw on the new betadine cap. Tighten it firmly.
Step 4
Secure & Move
Tuck your catheter into your pouch or belt. Check your total ultrafiltration (UF) on the machine and record it.
