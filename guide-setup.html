🧤 Procedure Guide

Daily Treatment Setup

Good preparation is 90% of a successful treatment. Follow these steps to ensure a safe, sterile start every evening.

Home › Treatment Setup

🏠 1. Prepare Your Environment

Before you even touch your supplies, you must ensure your "Clean Zone" is ready.

  • Close windows and doors to prevent drafts that carry invisible dust and bacteria.
  • Turn off fans or air conditioners in the room.
  • Clean your workspace (table or nightstand) using a disinfectant spray or wipe.
  • Keep pets out of the room during the entire setup and connection process.
⚠️

Mask up! Both you and any helper in the room should wear a clean surgical mask once you begin handling the sterile components.

📦 2. Gather Your Supplies

Check your prescription and gather everything you'll need for tonight.

  • Dialysate bags (check concentration, expiration date, and volume).
  • APD Cassette in its sterile packaging.
  • Disconnect caps (betadine caps).
  • Organiser (to hold your tubing in place).
  • Hand sanitizer and surface disinfectant.
💡

Check the "Go" signal. Examine your solution bags. The fluid should be perfectly clear. If it's cloudy or the bag has a leak (check the outer wrap for moisture), do not use it.

🧼 3. The 2-Minute Hand Wash

This is the most important step in preventing infection. Hand sanitizer is great, but it doesn't replace a thorough wash.

Wet & Soap
Wet hands with warm water and apply antimicrobial soap.
The "Everything" Scrub
Scrub palms, backs of hands, between fingers, under fingernails (use a nail brush if needed), and up to your wrists.
Full 2 Minutes
Keep scrubbing for at least 120 seconds. Sing "Happy Birthday" slowly four times.
Dry Sterilely
Rinse well and dry with a fresh paper towel. Use the towel to turn off the tap.

⚙️ 4. Set Up the Cycler

Now you're ready to interact with the HomeChoice APD Cycler.

  • Turn on the machine (switch on the back).
  • Follow the screen prompts. The machine will perform a self-test.
  • Load the cassette. Open the door, slide the cassette in, and close the door firmly. You'll hear the motor engage.
  • Connect solution bags. Use your sterile technique to connect the heater bag and additional bags to the red and white lines.
  • Prime the system. Press the button to let the machine fill the tubing with solution, removing all air.
📱

Wait for the "Connect Patient" prompt. Do not attempt to connect yourself until the machine has finished priming and the screen explicitly tells you it's time. See this in 3D AR →
