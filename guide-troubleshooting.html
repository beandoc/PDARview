Troubleshooting Alarms

The cycler uses alarms to keep you safe. Most alarms are simple fixes that you can handle yourself without worry.

Home › Troubleshooting

📉 Alarm: Low Drain / Slow Flow

This is the most common alarm. It means the solution isn't draining out of your abdomen at the expected speed.

Common Causes & Fixes:

  • Kinked tubing: Check the line from your catheter to the machine. Ensure you aren't laying on it.
  • Constipation: If your bowels are full, they can press against the catheter. Take your prescribed stool softener.
  • Position: Sometimes the catheter tip is resting against the wall of your peritoneum. Try rolling onto your other side or sitting up.
  • Bag height: Ensure your drain bag is sitting lower than your body level.
💡

Try the "PD Roll". Gently turn from side to side in bed. This often shifts the fluid and allows the drain to resume automatically.

👀 Alarm: Check Lines / Air in Line

The machine has detected a blockage or a small air bubble in the tubing.

  • Check all clamps: Ensure all white clamps on the solution bags are OPEN.
  • Check your transfer set: Ensure your twist-clamp is fully OPEN.
  • Look for bubbles: If you see a large bubble, follow the machine prompts to "Bypass" or "Re-prime" if necessary. Small bubbles are normal and caught by the machine's air trap.

🔌 Alarm: Power Failure

If the power goes out during your treatment:

Don't Panic
The HomeChoice APD Cycler has an internal memory. It will remember exactly where you were in your treatment.
Clamp & Relax
Clamp your transfer set and wait for the power to return if you expect it soon.
Resume
Once power returns, the machine will ask if you want to "Resume" or "End Therapy". Choose Resume.
Important
Prolonged Outage
If power is out for more than 2 hours, call your PD nurse. You may need to do a manual exchange (CAPD) to stay on schedule.

⚠️ System Error (Exxxx)

If you see a code starting with 'E' (e.g., E304):

  1. Write down the exact code.
  2. Turn the machine off, wait 10 seconds, and turn it back on.
  3. If the error persists, do not use the machine. Call the technical support number on the side of the cycler.

Still Need Help?

If an alarm won't clear or you feel unwell, contact your care team immediately.
