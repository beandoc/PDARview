Common Questions

What Patients Ask

The most common concerns about starting peritoneal dialysis.

📏 Will the machine fit on my nightstand? ▾

The Claria is about the size of a large toaster — 46.7 cm wide × 38.7 cm deep × 19.4 cm tall. Most nightstands have plenty of room. Use our 3D viewer to place it in AR on your actual furniture and see for yourself!

🔊 How loud is it at night? ▾

The Claria operates very quietly — most patients compare it to a gentle hum, similar to a fish tank filter. It also features an auto-dim screen so it won't light up your bedroom.

💉 Does peritoneal dialysis hurt? ▾

PD itself is painless. You may feel a sensation of fullness when the solution fills your abdomen, but most patients get used to this quickly. There are no needles involved — the dialysate flows through a soft catheter. The initial catheter placement surgery involves some recovery discomfort, but this subsides within 1–2 weeks.

🦠 What about infection risk? ▾

The main risk is peritonitis (abdominal infection), but it's highly preventable with good hand hygiene and sterile connection technique. Most experienced PD patients have 1 episode or fewer every 2–3 years. Learn about infection prevention →

✈️ Can I travel on PD? ▾

Yes! Many patients travel domestically and internationally. Your supply company ships to your destination. For CAPD, you don't even need the machine — just bags and tubing. Read our travel guide →

💼 Can I work or study while on PD? ▾

Absolutely. With APD, your treatment happens overnight while you sleep. Your days are completely free for work, school, or anything else. Many patients report less disruption to their professional life than hemodialysis patients. See daily life on PD →
