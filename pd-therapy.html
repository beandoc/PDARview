🔁 What you're watching

1 · Fill (~10 min)
Warmed, sterile dialysate flows down the Tenckhoff catheter into your peritoneal cavity — about 2 litres for an adult.
2 · Dwell (~4 hours)
The fluid rests against the peritoneal membrane. Wastes (urea, creatinine) and excess water cross from your blood into the fluid — the moving particles. Glucose in the fluid pulls the extra water out.
3 · Drain (~20 min)
The now-cloudy, waste-laden fluid drains out, and a fresh fill begins. On APD the cycler repeats this automatically overnight.

💡 Why it matters

Your peritoneum is a living filter. The clearer you can picture the fill–dwell–drain cycle, the easier it is to understand why dwell times, fluid strengths and clean technique all matter — and to spot when something (like cloudy drain fluid) is a warning sign worth calling your team about.
