📝 Storage Best Practices

✅ Proper Storage

  • FIFO: First-In, First-Out. Use oldest bags first.
  • Off the Floor: Use a pallet or shelf to prevent moisture damage.
  • Temperature: Keep between 15°C–30°C (59°F–86°F).

❌ Avoid These

  • Direct Sunlight: Degrades fluid and plastic over time.
  • Damp Areas: Mold can form on outer boxes and contaminate connections.
  • Extreme Cold: Freezing causes bags to leak or burst.
